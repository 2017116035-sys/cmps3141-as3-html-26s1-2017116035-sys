Dark Pattern UI/UX (Safety)

URL: https://hallofshame.design/groww-checkbox-preselection-deceptive-pattern/

I Like…

  • The prominent "Get started" call-to-action button and straightforward "Pre-approved loan ₹ 5,00,000" displayed clear.
  • Transparently stating terms and policies directly adjacent to the main user action area.

I Wish…

  • The app didn't automatically pre-check the consent checkbox for checking user's credit score before they tap "Get started".
  • By default, pre-selected consent checkboxes tricked users into authorizing credit score checks without thinking, risking their financial standing as each check impacted their score.

What If…

  • Require users to explicitly interact with and manually check the consent box before enabling the "Get started" button.
  • Display a clear, prominent notice explaining that checking the credit score may impact their credit rating prior to requesting permission.
